Boss guide

Companion to the Minion Army guide — fight-by-fight tactics, written for this build (minions do the damage, you stay alive).

Boss guide v1.1 · 2026-11-12 · patch 0.5.5
← Minion guide
  • Where: the Burning Monolith, inside the Precursor Fortress. Your first kill is the quest version (fragments from the two Enigma Chambers), and it gives unlimited revivals — use it to practise. Repeat kills need three Crisis Fragments from Citadels.
  • Health: about 5 million. At about 55% he turns immune, takes his sword and starts phase 2 — and heals back to full, so phase 1 is only the first half.
  • Damage: mostly fire, plus physical sword hits. When he flashes red with a sound, that attack must be dodged — no amount of resistance helps.
  • Your job: survive. Your minions do the damage whether or not you're attacking.

Raise your maximum fire resistance. Your fire resistance is already 80% before the cap, so every +1% maximum counts straight away:

Together: about 82% instead of 75% — roughly 28% less fire damage taken. Guides recommend 80–85%. (The tree notable Unnatural Resilience is 22 points away — not worth it.)

  • Charms: Thawing, Stone, and Beira's Anguish instead of Silver — dying to ignite between dodges is a common way to lose this fight.
  • Minions: if you run Frost Mages (with Rakiata's Flow), swap them for Skeletal Reavers for this fight. Chilling this boss can make his beam and Flame Seed combo unavoidable (a reported bug), and the Frost Mage chills. Your guide's default is already the Reaver.
  • Health: guides suggest 3,500–4,000+ effective health for the hits you can't avoid; you have about 7,000 (Energy Shield + unreserved Life).
  1. Dodge-roll comes first. When a mechanic starts, stop everything else.
  2. W — Pain Offering: keep it up. It's the one button worth pressing between mechanics.
  3. Q (Shattering Roar) and right-click (Vulnerability): only in safe windows — for example while he channels his Fire Beam.
  4. R — Detonate Dead when corpses pile up; then E (Unearth).
  5. Right after the phase-2 transition: refresh W, Q and right-click.
▸ What resistance can and can't fix. Can't: red-flash attacks, standing outside a Flame Seed ring, Hallways (fire winds) — all one-shots — and the physical part of sword hits. Can: fire projectiles, beams, burning ground, ignite, and the phase-2 Baiting Flame Seeds damage (guides measured about 3,500 at 85% maximum fire resistance — survivable for you).
Fire Beam
fire — reducible
He channels a straight beam. Walk sideways out of its line. While he channels, it's a safe moment for Q and right-click.
Fire projectiles
fire — reducible
Slow single fireballs. Sidestep them.
Bullet Hell orbs
fire — reducible
Two fire orbs spray small projectiles across the arena — several at once hurt badly. Move past or behind the orbs: the projectiles keep flying towards where you were.
Flame Seed
one-shot
A seed falls into a marked ring. Walk into the ring before it lands — everything outside explodes. He keeps attacking meanwhile, so stay calm and get inside.
Cascading Flame Seeds
one-shot
He dashes around dropping several rings that explode one after another. Go from ring to ring, moving right after each blast.
Hallways / Flaming Gales — the fire winds
one-shot
He rises and fire blasts cover almost the whole arena, leaving one narrow safe lane. More resistance won't save you — only the lane does.
  1. Listen for his voice line — it now plays as Hallways begins. Treat it as "stop everything".
  2. Look for the faint blue tint — that marks the safe lane between the blasts.
  3. Commit — don't hover at the edge. Hesitating on the border is the classic death.
  4. Stand at mid-range, not at the arena's edge — near the middle, any lane is a short run away.
  5. Keep your dodge-roll ready — walk for routine dodging; the roll is your fastest way to a far lane.
  6. Stand away from your army — dozens of skeletons and explosions hide a faint tint.
  7. Practise just this on the quest version: unlimited revivals, ignore his health.
The transition
At about 55% he becomes immune, goes to the centre and takes his sword — and heals to full. Don't waste buttons into the immunity; reposition to mid-range and refresh W, Q and right-click.
Sword slashes and combos
physical — not reduced by fire res
Keep moving around him and out of the sword's reach. Fire resistance does nothing for the physical part.
Sword Beam
He points the sword and fires a long beam. Get out of the line the sword points along first, then stay in a clear area.
Baiting Flame Seeds — the hardest attack
reducible with ~85% max fire res
He drops two rings right in front of him, fires his Sword Beam over the first and slams the second. Stay just outside the first ring until the beam fires, then step in. For the second, stand to the side of the ring so the slam's line misses you. With high maximum fire resistance and your Energy Shield you can survive it even when the timing beats you.
Chill and Blackflame Seeds
A white and a black orb appear. When he uses one (chilled or burning ground across the arena), step on the other to cancel it.
Pulsing Orb — ring of fire projectiles
fire — reducible
A ring of projectiles spreads outward; if one hits you or a minion, the whole ring explodes. Move away from your army and pass through a visible gap.
Diving Slam
He dives at a ring's safe spot. Wait for the dive to land, step into the safe area, then dodge away from the follow-up slash.
