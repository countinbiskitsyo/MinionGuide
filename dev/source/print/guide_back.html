PoB cannot see how often it uses that skill: watch it on a boss. It's also very slow (move speed 11 vs a median 32): for mapping, Nymph Wasp (skill 3) does 65% of the damage at 2.3× the speed.
▸ Gem rules: Support gems have NO level — they use tiers (I/II/III) set when you engrave them. Only active & spirit gems have levels, raised by consuming an Uncut Skill Gem of that level or higher (gems never gain XP). Vendors do not sell gems. Uncut gem level = area level, so farm the highest zone you can clear; side bosses (coloured skull icons) are the best campaign source. Moving a support between skills is free.
▸ Army by Spirit: ~30–60 Warriors + Sniper · ~100 add Spectre · ~140 add Brute (Intimidate +10% army dmg) · ~170 add Frost Mage (Command shields minions) · spare 16+: Skeletal Cleric · Discipline if it fits.
▸ Socket order while you only have 2–3 sockets: Warrior → Feeding Frenzy I 1.45× then Brutality III · Sniper → Feeding Frenzy I 1.41× then Brutality III · Brute → Feeding Frenzy I 1.39× then Bleed III · Spectre → Concentrated Area then Feeding Frenzy I · Mages → Feeding Frenzy I then Muster (1.42×) (Dex 7 pays for one Dex support — save it for Rakiata's Flow). Jeweller's Orb: Lesser→3 sockets, Greater→4, Perfect→5.
