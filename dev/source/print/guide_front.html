▸ Buy order on a budget: ① Sceptre (Spirit% + minion levels) ② Amulet (+minion levels, +Spirit) ③ Body (+Spirit) ④ chaos res spread across helmet/gloves/belt/rings ⑤ boots last. Buy one strong affix per item — dual-stat rares cost far more than the sum of their parts.
▸ While levelling: the roll ranges are endgame ceilings — low item-level gear rolls the same stats lower, so take them anyway. Until your 40s: Spirit & minion levels → Life (and Str) → resistances. ES only climbs to the top after the tree's ES block (~pt 49) and Beidat's Hand (~Lv 70). +Spirit stops mattering once your army fits.
④ ROTATION & TROUBLESHOOTING
Mapping
  1. Unearth
  2. Pain Offering
  3. Detonate Dead
  4. Vulnerability
  5. Walk behind the army
Bossing
  1. Unearth → Pain Offering
  2. Despair
  3. Brute Command (Intimidate +10%)
  4. Detonate Dead
  5. Frost Mage Command last
⚠ Corpse economy: Unearth and Detonate Dead both consume corpses and compete (Pain Offering does not). Corpses come from your own minions dying & reviving — so both are weakest at the START of a boss fight. Sacrifice (Spirit gem) lets living minions count as corpses — the fix for single-target.
Stuck on a boss? ① minions dying → add Skeletal Cleric or more bodies ② you dying → resistances, then Life; keep your distance ③ boss not dying → raise active gem levels, then Brute Command, then a better spectre ④ still stuck → go get Ascendancy points.
