▸ While levelling: the “best roll” column is the endgame ceiling — low item-level gear rolls the same stats lower, so take them anyway. Priorities (measured on the finished Lv 100 tree) shift early: until your 40s go Spirit & minion levels → Life (and Str) → resistances. ES only climbs to the top after the tree's ES block (~pt 49) and Beidat's Hand (~Lv 70). +Spirit stops mattering once your army fits.
